Student Stress Check-in

Multimodal (questionnaire, daily habits, optional voice) stress/risk indicator — experimental research demonstration.

Not a medical tool. This is a non-clinical research and early-intervention demonstration. It does not diagnose any condition and does not replace a counsellor, psychologist or doctor. If you are struggling or feel unsafe, please contact your campus counselling service or a local helpline.

Consent & privacy

  • Everything is optional — share only what you are comfortable with.
  • Your answers are sent to the assessment service running on this computer, used to compute the result, and not stored. Nothing is saved by this page, and results are not cached by the browser.
  • A voice recording is held in memory in your browser, sent once, analysed from a temporary file that is deleted immediately, and never kept. Nothing is uploaded to any external service.
  • Results are indicators from small, experimental models (see “How these models were evaluated”).
