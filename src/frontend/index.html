Student stress check-in

Answer a 20-question survey and five questions about your daily habits, and optionally add a short voice recording. The tool turns what it can use into a low, moderate or high stress indicator, shows which of your own answers pointed to it, and suggests specific next steps.

Start the check-in How it works

Research demonstration. It is not a medical test, and it runs on this computer without storing your answers.

What you will be asked

Questionnaire
20 questions about the past week: workload, concentration, sleep, mood and how you cope.
Daily habits
5 numbers: sleep, study routine, social time, screen time and routine regularity.
Voice (optional)
A recording of at least 5 seconds, or a WAV file.

How the result is produced

Each source is scored by its own model. The scores are then combined, weighting each one by its confidence and how complete its data is. A source that is missing or fails is left out and reported as such; it is never guessed.

1.Questionnaire

A random forest trained on a small set of real survey responses. Its results are preliminary.

2.Daily habits

A logistic regression trained on synthetic data. Treat its scores as a demonstration, not as evidence.

3.Voice

There is no voice stress model, because no stress-labelled recordings exist. A separate, experimental read-out of acted emotions may appear. It is not a stress measure and is not used in the result.

With two or more sources, the tool also compares them. If the questionnaire is noticeably lower than your daily habits, it flags a possible masked-stress pattern as something to reflect on. Accuracy figures for every model are under Evaluation, below.

Consent and privacy

What happens with your answers.

Not a medical tool. This is a non-clinical research and early-intervention demonstration. It does not diagnose any condition and does not replace a counsellor, psychologist or doctor. If you are struggling or feel unsafe, please contact your campus counselling service or a local helpline.
  • Everything is optional: share only what you are comfortable with.
  • Your answers go only to the assessment service running on this computer to compute the result, and are not stored. Nothing is saved by this page, and results are not cached by the browser.
  • A voice recording is held in memory in your browser, sent once, analysed from a temporary file that is deleted immediately, and never kept.
  • If you tick “Analyse what I say”, your recording is also sent to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to be turned into text, and that text to Azure AI Language to estimate its tone. Neither the audio nor the text is stored by this app.
  • If you tick “Save to my history”, an anonymised summary (date, scores, levels and factor names; never your answers, habits, voice or transcript) is stored in Azure Cosmos DB under a random ID kept in this browser. It is deleted automatically after 180 days, or straight away with “Delete my history”.
  • Results come from small, experimental models (see Evaluation below).
